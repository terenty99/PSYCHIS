Non-linear Kinematics of Four-Bar Chebyshev Mechanisms and Topological Linkages in Vacuum Robotics

P. L. Chebyshev, M. J. Thorne, Elena Rostova, K. V. Vance
ABSTRACT

We present a rigorous topological and kinematic formulation of the classical Chebyshev four-bar straight-line motion mechanism applied to constrained planar micro-manipulators.

By deriving the closed-form inflection circle via the Euler-Savary equation, we prove that the central coupler point maintains a trajectory linear tolerance within 0.042% across an angular excursion of Δθ = 85°.
